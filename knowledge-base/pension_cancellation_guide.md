# Pension Cancellation and Family Pension Claim Guide — India
> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — For demonstration purposes only. Not real legal advice.

## Overview
When a government employee/pensioner dies, two parallel actions are needed:
1. Cancel the deceased's pension immediately (prevent overpayment)
2. Start Family Pension for eligible dependants

## Applicable Pension Schemes
- Central Government: CCS (Pension) Rules 2021
- State Government: Respective State Service Rules
- Railways: Railway Services (Pension) Rules
- Defence: Defence Services Regulations
- Teachers: State-specific pension rules
- Employees covered under NPS (National Pension System): Different rules apply

## Step 1: Cancellation of Deceased's Pension

### Who to Notify
- Pension Disbursing Authority (PDA): Bank that pays pension
- Principal Accounts Office of the ministry/department
- Last employer/department of the deceased

### Documents for Cancellation
- Death Certificate
- PPO (Pension Payment Order) — original booklet
- Pensioner's bank passbook
- Letter from family requesting cancellation and family pension initiation

### Overpayment Issue
If pension was credited after date of death:
- Bank recovers excess amount from deceased's account
- If account is insufficient: family must refund the overpaid amount
- Keep records of date of death vs pension credit dates

## Step 2: Family Pension Claim

### Who is Eligible for Family Pension (Central Govt)
1. **Spouse** — for life (or remarriage)
2. **Children** — till age 25 (or marriage, whichever earlier)
3. **Disabled children** — for life regardless of age/marriage
4. **Parents** — if no surviving spouse/children and parents were dependent

### Enhanced Family Pension (First 7 Years or till Age 67)
- 50% of last pay drawn by deceased
- Available if: employee died in service OR pensioner died within 7 years of retirement AND before age 67

### Normal Family Pension (After Enhanced Period)
- 30% of last pay drawn
- Minimum: ₹9,000/month (7th Pay Commission)

### Documents Required for Family Pension
- Death Certificate
- Original PPO (Pension Payment Order)
- Family Pension Application Form (Form 14 — Central Govt)
- Proof of relationship (Marriage Certificate / Birth Certificate)
- Joint photograph of claimant and deceased (if available)
- DCRG (Death-cum-Retirement Gratuity) claim form
- Bank details of claimant (for payment)
- No re-marriage declaration by spouse

## Step-by-Step Process
1. Submit all documents to Head of Office (last employer)
2. Head of Office verifies and forwards to Pay & Accounts Office
3. Revised PPO issued in family pensioner's name
4. Bank notified to update pension payment
5. Family pension starts within 1–3 months of complete submission

## NPS (National Pension System) Death Benefit
- Entire NPS corpus paid to nominee
- No annuity mandatory (unlike regular retirement)
- Apply through employer or NPS CRA (Central Record Keeping Agency): npscra.nsdl.co.in
- Documents: Death Certificate, Subscriber ID, Nominee's KYC

## State Pension Schemes
- Each state has its own forms and PAO offices
- Process is similar to Central Govt but with state-specific forms
- Contact the State Treasury Office or Pay & Accounts Office
