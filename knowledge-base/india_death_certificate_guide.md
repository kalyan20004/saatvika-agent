# India Death Certificate Registration Guide
> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — For demonstration purposes only. Not real legal advice.

## Overview
A Death Certificate is a mandatory legal document issued by the Municipal Corporation or Gram Panchayat. Registration of death is compulsory under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969.

## Key Deadlines
- Deaths must be registered within **21 days** of occurrence.
- After 21 days but within 30 days: late fee applies.
- After 30 days: requires Executive Magistrate or SDM order.
- After 1 year: requires order from a First Class Judicial Magistrate.

## Who Can Register
- Head of household where death occurred
- Doctor/Medical Officer in charge of the institution
- Prison in-charge (if death in prison)
- Village Panchayat head (for rural areas)

## Documents Required
- Proof of Death (hospital discharge summary / doctor's certificate)
- Proof of Address of deceased (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Ration Card)
- Proof of Age (Birth Certificate, School Certificate, Passport)
- Informant's ID proof (Aadhaar card)
- Marriage certificate (if applicable, for spouse)

## Step-by-Step Process
1. Obtain a Medical Certificate of Cause of Death (MCCD) from the hospital/doctor.
2. Visit the local municipal office or Gram Panchayat within 21 days.
3. Fill Form 2 (Death Report form under RBD Act).
4. Submit with all supporting documents and pay nominal fee (₹10–50 depending on state).
5. Collect acknowledgment slip.
6. Certificate issued within 7 working days.
7. Apply for additional certified copies (needed for banks, insurance, courts).

## Number of Copies Required
- Banks: 1 copy each per account
- Insurance companies: 1 copy each
- EPF/Pension: 1 copy
- Property/Mutation: 2 copies
- Court/Probate: 1 copy
- Total recommended: **Obtain 10 certified copies**

## Online Application
- National: crsorgi.gov.in (Central Registration Office)
- Most states now have online portals — search '[State] death certificate online application'

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Missing the 21-day deadline
- Spelling errors in deceased's name (must match other documents exactly)
- Not obtaining enough copies
- Not getting the cause of death certified by a registered medical practitioner
