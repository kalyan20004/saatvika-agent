# India EPF/EPS Withdrawal After Death of Member Guide
> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — For demonstration purposes only. Not real legal advice.

## Overview
If an EPF (Employees Provident Fund) member dies, the nominee or legal heir is entitled to:
1. EPF Accumulations (employee + employer contribution + interest)
2. EPS (Employee Pension Scheme) benefits
3. EDLI (Employees Deposit Linked Insurance) — insurance benefit up to ₹7 lakh

## Who Can Claim
- Registered nominee (Form 2 filed with employer)
- If no nominee: legal heir with succession certificate

## EPF Death Claim — Documents Required
- Form 20 (for EPF balance claim)
- Form 10-D (for monthly EPS pension) OR Form 10-C (for EPS withdrawal if less than 10 years service)
- Form 5-IF (for EDLI claim)
- Death Certificate (original)
- Nominee's Aadhaar card
- Nominee's PAN card
- Nominee's bank passbook copy (with IFSC)
- Member's PF account number / UAN
- Employer's attestation (if member was in service)
- Guardianship certificate (if nominee is minor)

## Step-by-Step Process
1. Get UAN (Universal Account Number) of deceased member from employer or EPFO portal.
2. Download Forms 20, 10-D/10-C, and 5-IF from epfindia.gov.in.
3. Fill all forms and get employer attestation on Form 20.
4. Submit at regional EPFO office OR upload online via employer login.
5. EPFO processes within 30 days.
6. EPF amount credited to nominee's bank account.
7. EDLI insurance (Form 5-IF) credited separately.

## Online Claim Submission
- Employer can submit claim online via Unified Portal: unifiedportal-emp.epfindia.gov.in
- Nominee needs UAN activated and linked to Aadhaar

## EPS Pension (Monthly Pension for Family)
- Eligible if deceased had 10+ years of service
- Spouse gets monthly pension (Form 10-D)
- Children eligible till age 25
- If less than 10 years service: lump sum withdrawal (Form 10-C)

## EDLI Insurance Amount
- Minimum: ₹2.5 lakh
- Maximum: ₹7 lakh
- Calculated based on last 12 months' salary
- No premium paid by employee — employer contribution

## Gratuity (Separate from EPF)
- If deceased was in service for 5+ years
- Employer must pay gratuity to nominee within 30 days of death
- Maximum: ₹20 lakh
- Claim from employer directly with Death Certificate + Form I

## If Employer Denies Cooperation
- File grievance at epfigms.gov.in
- Contact regional EPFO Enforcement Officer
