# Legal Heir Certificate Guide — India
> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — For demonstration purposes only. Not real legal advice.

## What is a Legal Heir Certificate?
A Legal Heir Certificate (also called Varisu Certificate in Tamil Nadu or Vaarisu Praman Patra in Hindi) is issued by the Revenue Department to identify all surviving legal heirs of the deceased.

## When is it Needed?
- Claiming pension, gratuity from employer
- EPF/ESIC claims
- Bank account transfer (as supporting document)
- Property mutation at tehsildar's office
- Employment compassionate appointment
- Government schemes for deceased's dependants

## Legal Heir Certificate vs Succession Certificate
| Aspect | Legal Heir Certificate | Succession Certificate |
|---|---|---|
| Issued by | Revenue Department (Tehsildar) | District Court |
| Purpose | Identify legal heirs | Authorize heir to collect assets |
| Time | 15–30 days | 3–6 months |
| Cost | ₹50 – ₹500 | 2–3% of asset value as court fee |
| Validity | For government claims | For bank, financial claims |

## Documents Required for Legal Heir Certificate
- Application form (available at Tehsildar office or e-District portal)
- Death Certificate of deceased (original + 2 copies)
- Proof of relationship:
  - Spouse: Marriage Certificate
  - Children: Birth Certificates
  - Parents: School records / Birth Certificate of deceased
- Address proof of all legal heirs (Aadhaar)
- Ration Card (if available — shows family members)
- Affidavit on stamp paper (₹20–100) declaring all legal heirs
- 2 passport photos of applicant

## Step-by-Step Process
1. Obtain application form from Tehsildar's office or e-District portal
2. Fill all legal heirs' details (name, relationship, age, Aadhaar)
3. Get affidavit notarized on stamp paper
4. Submit at Tehsildar's office with all documents
5. Revenue Inspector verifies by visiting residence
6. Tehsildar issues certificate within 15–30 days

## Who Counts as Legal Heir?
**For Hindus (Hindu Succession Act 1956):**
- Class I Heirs (highest priority): Spouse, Sons, Daughters, Mother, Son's widow, Son's children
- Class II Heirs: Father, siblings, grandchildren (inherit only if no Class I heirs)

**For Muslims:**
- Governed by personal law; shares are prescribed (1/8 for spouse if children exist, etc.)

**For Christians/Parsis:**
- Indian Succession Act 1925 applies

## Validity Period
- Most government departments accept certificates without time limit
- Some departments require fresh certificate if older than 1–2 years
