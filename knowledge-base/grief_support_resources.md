## What to Expect in Grief
Grief is not linear. The stages (denial, anger, bargaining, depression, acceptance) may occur in any order or repeat. 

**In the first weeks:**
- Practical tasks will feel overwhelming — that is normal
- Accept help from friends and family for household tasks
- Do not make major financial decisions for at least 3–6 months

**Talking to Children About Death:**
- Use clear, honest language appropriate for age
- Maintain routines as much as possible
- School counsellors can help — inform the school

## Self-Care Reminders for Family Members
- Eat regular meals even if appetite is low
- Sleep — grief is physically exhausting
- Avoid alcohol as a coping mechanism
- One task per day is enough in the first weeks
- It is okay to ask for help with administrative tasks
