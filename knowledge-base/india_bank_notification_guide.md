# India Bank Account Notification After Death Guide
> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — For demonstration purposes only. Not real legal advice.

## Overview
Upon the death of a bank account holder, surviving family members must notify all banks to prevent fraud, claim funds, and either transfer or close accounts. RBI Master Circular on Customer Service governs this process.

## Types of Bank Accounts and Procedures

### 1. Single Account (No Nominee)
- Funds go to legal heir(s)
- Required: Succession Certificate or Letters of Administration from court
- Small amounts (under ₹1 lakh, varies by bank): Banks may use affidavit + indemnity bond
- Large amounts: Court order mandatory

### 2. Single Account (With Nominee)
- Nominee can claim funds
- Required: Death Certificate + Nominee's ID + Claim Form
- Nominee is trustee for legal heirs (not absolute owner)
- Process: 2–4 weeks

### 3. Joint Account (Either or Survivor)
- Surviving account holder continues normally
- Notify bank of co-holder's death with Death Certificate
- Account continues in survivor's name

### 4. Joint Account (Both/All to Sign)
- Account freezes on death of one holder
- Requires legal heir process similar to single account

## Documents Required
- Original Death Certificate + 2 photocopies
- Nominee's / Legal Heir's photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN)
- Nominee's / Legal Heir's address proof
- Filled bank claim form (available at branch)
- Passbook / cheque book of deceased's account
- PAN card of deceased (if available)
- For large amounts: Succession Certificate, Will (if any)

## Step-by-Step Process
1. Visit the home branch of each bank with documents.
2. Submit written notice of death with Death Certificate.
3. Request to freeze account immediately to prevent unauthorized transactions.
4. Submit claim form (nominee or legal heir).
5. Bank will verify documents (7–30 days).
6. Funds transferred to nominee/heir's account.
7. Account closed after transfer.

## Fixed Deposits (FDs)
- Premature closure allowed on death — no penalty
- Interest calculated till date of death
- Nominee/heir can choose to continue FD till maturity

## Locker Access
- Joint locker: surviving holder can access
- Sole locker: Only legal heir with Succession Certificate + court order
- Bank inventory of locker contents is mandatory

## Unclaimed Deposits
- If not claimed within 10 years, transferred to RBI's Depositor Education and Awareness Fund (DEAF)
- Can be reclaimed from RBI even after transfer

## Key RBI Guidelines
- Banks must settle claims within 15 days of receiving complete documents (for claims under ₹5 lakh)
- Banks cannot insist on Succession Certificate for amounts under the threshold if there is a nominee
- Grievance: File complaint with Banking Ombudsman if bank delays beyond 15 days

## Checklist Per Account
- [ ] Notify bank of death in writing
- [ ] Submit Death Certificate
- [ ] Submit claim form
- [ ] Submit ID proof
- [ ] Confirm account freeze
- [ ] Track claim status
- [ ] Collect funds / transfer
- [ ] Confirm account closure
