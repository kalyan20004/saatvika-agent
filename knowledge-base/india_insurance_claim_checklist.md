# India Life Insurance Claim Checklist
> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — For demonstration purposes only. Not real legal advice.

## Overview
Life insurance death claims must be filed promptly. IRDAI regulations require insurers to settle admitted claims within 30 days of receiving all documents.

## Types of Claims
1. **Early Claim**: Death within 3 years of policy issuance — requires detailed investigation
2. **Non-Early Claim**: Death after 3 years — simpler process
3. **Accidental Claim**: Additional investigation, FIR/inquest report required

## LIC (Life Insurance Corporation) Claim Process

### Step 1: Intimate LIC Branch
- Visit the servicing branch or nearest LIC branch
- Submit written intimation of death with policy number

### Step 2: Documents Required (LIC)
- Original Policy Bond
- Death Certificate (original)
- Claimant's Statement (Form 3784 for non-accidental / Form 3801 for accidental)
- Age proof of deceased
- Nominee/Heir's ID and address proof
- Nominee/Heir's bank account details (cancelled cheque / passbook)
- Medical attendant's certificate (Form 3785) — if hospitalized
- Hospital certificate (Form 3816) — if hospitalized
- For accidental death: FIR, Post-Mortem report, Inquest report

### Step 3: LIC Investigation (Early Claims)
- Branch investigates thoroughly for policies under 3 years
- May contact hospital, doctors, employer
- Timeline: 60–90 days for early claims

### Step 4: Claim Settlement
- Claim approved: Amount credited to nominee's bank account
- Claim repudiated: Written explanation mandatory, can appeal to IRDAI

## Private Insurer Claim Process
- Similar to LIC but forms vary by company
- Most private insurers have online claim portals
- Common private insurers: HDFC Life, ICICI Prudential, Max Life, SBI Life, Bajaj Allianz

## PMJJBY (Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana)
- Government scheme, coverage ₹2 lakh
- Claim through nominee's bank
- Documents: Death Certificate + Claim Form (available at bank)
- Settlement: 30 days

## If No Nominee is Registered
- Policy proceeds go to legal heirs
- Requires Succession Certificate from court
- Timeline significantly increases (3–6 months)

## IRDAI Grievance
- If insurer delays beyond 30 days without cause: file complaint at igms.irda.gov.in
- Insurance Ombudsman offices in major cities

## Checklist
- [ ] Locate all insurance policy documents
- [ ] Note each policy's servicing branch
- [ ] Intimate each insurer in writing
- [ ] Gather required forms from each insurer
- [ ] Submit complete documentation
- [ ] Track claim reference number
- [ ] Follow up at 30-day mark
- [ ] Confirm settlement to nominee's bank
