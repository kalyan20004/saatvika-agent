# India Probate Process Guide
> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — For demonstration purposes only. Not real legal advice.

## What is Probate?
Probate is a court process to validate a Will and establish the executor's legal authority to distribute the estate.

## When is Probate Mandatory?
- **Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata**: Mandatory for all Wills
- **Other states**: Probate is optional but recommended if:
  - Will is likely to be contested
  - Property value is high
  - Multiple heirs with potential disputes
  - Property is in Mumbai/Chennai/Kolkata even if owner lived elsewhere

## When is Probate NOT Required?
- No Will exists (intestate) — use Succession Certificate instead
- Property is in states where probate is not mandatory and heirs agree
- Jointly held property passing to survivor

## Probate Process — Step by Step

### Step 1: File Petition
- File petition in the District Court / High Court of the district where deceased last resided
- Include: Original Will, Death Certificate, list of assets, list of heirs
- Petition by executor named in Will (or any interested party)

### Step 2: Court Issues Notice
- Court issues notice to all legal heirs
- Public citation in newspaper (for unknown heirs)
- Objection period: 30 days

### Step 3: Hearing
- If no objections: Court grants Probate within 3–6 months
- If contested: Full trial required — can take 1–5 years

### Step 4: Probate Granted
- Court issues Probate (certified copy of Will with court seal)
- Executor now has legal authority to administer estate
- Executor pays debts, files taxes, distributes assets per Will

## Succession Certificate (When There's No Will)
- Issued for movable assets (bank accounts, shares, FDs, loans due)
- Applied in District Court of last residence of deceased
- Documents: Death Certificate, Petition, heir relationship proof
- Timeline: 3–6 months (uncontested)
- Cost: Court fee based on asset value (0.5–2% of estate)

## Letters of Administration
- For immovable property when there's no Will in non-probate states
- Similar process to Succession Certificate
- Grants administrator authority to manage estate

## Documents Required for Probate Petition
- Original Will
- Death Certificate
- Proof of relationship (birth certificates, marriage certificate)
- Property documents
- Bank statements showing assets
- Affidavit verifying Will's authenticity
- Advocate's vakalatnama

## Estimated Costs
- Court fees: ₹10,000 – ₹1,00,000+ (based on estate value)
- Advocate fees: ₹20,000 – ₹2,00,000+ (varies by city and complexity)
- Newspaper notice: ₹2,000 – ₹10,000
- Timeline (uncontested): 4–8 months
